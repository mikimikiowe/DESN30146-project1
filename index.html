Project 1 Concept

Project 1 Concept

Our concept is an interactive nature projection for hospital waiting areas that gives anxious patients a calm, optional distraction. A wall-sized forest or lake scene responds to movement, with leaves drifting, water rippling and wildlife appearing as people approach, alongside soft nature sounds. A Kinect or webcam tracks movement and sends it to TouchDesigner, which runs the visuals and sound in real time. We are following the Design path because our choices come from research on how nature imagery and sound reduce stress in care settings, and the project is shaped around a specific user.
